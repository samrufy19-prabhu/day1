How to prepare Rava Kaseri

Ingredients:

  • Roasted Rava
  • Roasted Cashew nuts
  • Water
  • Sugar
  • Ghee

Steps:

  1. Add a water into pan
  2. Pour a roasted rava into water once water gets boiled
  3. Stir the rava until it cooked
  4. Add sugar
  5. Stir it until sugar mixed with rava
  6. Finally add ghee and cashews
Sit and taste the yummy
